MING 2027

Call for Papers.

MING 2027 invites original, unpublished research on machine intelligence and next-generation computing. Topics include, but are not limited to, the following tracks.

01

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, large language models, reinforcement learning, explainable and trustworthy AI.

02

Data Science & Intelligent Information Processing

Data mining, big data analytics, knowledge representation, information retrieval, optimization, intelligent decision support.

03

Computer Vision & Intelligent Applications

Computer vision, pattern recognition, natural language processing, multimedia analysis, AI-enabled applications.

04

Cybersecurity, Privacy & Trustworthy Computing

Network security, privacy-preserving computing, cryptography, blockchain, digital forensics, secure AI systems.

05

Cloud, Edge, IoT & Distributed Computing

Cloud and edge intelligence, Internet of Things, distributed architectures, resource management, scalable computing.

06

Networks, Software & Emerging Computing

Communication networks, software engineering, cyber-physical systems, quantum computing, digital twins, smart systems.

07

AI for Interdisciplinary Applications

AI for healthcare and medical diagnosis, education and personalized learning, finance and FinTech, smart manufacturing, transportation and autonomous systems, smart cities, agriculture, environmental sustainability, and other interdisciplinary AI applications.

Submissions must be relevant to the conference scope and will undergo peer review. Paper submission deadline: April 30, 2027. Formatting requirements and the submission link will be announced later.
