MING 2027 / AUTHORS

Paper Submission.

MING 2027 invites original research contributions in machine intelligence and next-generation computing. Please review the requirements below before submitting your manuscript.

Paper Submission DeadlineFebruary 1, 2027
Revised Paper Submission DeadlineApril 1, 2027
Notification of AcceptanceMay 31, 2027

Author Guidelines.

Submitted manuscripts must present original work relevant to the conference topics and must not be published or under review elsewhere. Authors should ensure that all co-authors have approved the submission and that the paper accurately represents the research conducted.

Manuscripts must be written in English and submitted as a readable PDF. Papers should clearly describe their contributions, methodology, results, and relevant references. Authors are responsible for obtaining permission for any third-party material included in their papers.

Formatting template and page limits: Springer Smart Innovation, Systems and Technologies (SIST) is the intended proceedings series, subject to formal publisher approval. The final publisher-approved template, paper length requirements, and any supplementary material policy will be announced after publication arrangements are confirmed. Please do not assume a specific Springer template or page limit until it is announced by the organizers.

Submission Guidelines.

MING 2027 uses the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) for paper submission and peer review. The official conference site has been approved and is available at MING 2027 on Microsoft CMT. Please check the CMT site to confirm whether paper submission is currently open.

  1. Read the conference Call for Papers and confirm that your manuscript fits the scope.
  2. Prepare an English-language PDF and check the current author requirements on this page.
  3. Visit the official MING 2027 CMT site and confirm that paper submission is open. Create or sign in to a CMT account.
  4. Choose the appropriate subject area or track if prompted; enter the paper title, abstract, author information, and other required fields; upload the manuscript PDF.
  5. Review the information carefully and complete the submission before the deadline. Follow the CMT confirmation instructions.

Important: The April 1, 2027 revised-paper deadline is intended for revisions requested through the conference review process; it is not a substitute for the initial February 1 submission deadline. Detailed revision instructions will be provided by the organizers.

Submission deadline time zone and platform-specific requirements will be confirmed when the CMT conference site is configured.

Before submitting, create a Microsoft CMT account if you do not already have one. For step-by-step instructions, see the official Microsoft CMT author submission guide.
