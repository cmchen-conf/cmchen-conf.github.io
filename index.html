01 / THE CONFERENCE

Welcome to
MING 2027.

MING 2027, the International Conference on Machine Intelligence and Next-Generation Computing, aims to bring together researchers, academics, and practitioners to exchange advances in artificial intelligence, intelligent information processing, and emerging computing technologies.

The conference welcomes original research spanning foundational methods, computing systems, and real-world applications. MING seeks to foster rigorous scholarly exchange across interconnected areas of computing.

Submit your research.

MING 2027 welcomes original, unpublished research papers within the conference scope. Submissions will be handled through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT), using the official MING 2027 CMT conference site.
